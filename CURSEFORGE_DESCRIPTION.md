# 🎲 Universal Randomizer

**Universal Randomizer** is the ultimate, highly configurable, and seamless randomizer mod built for **Minecraft 1.20.1+** supporting both **Forge** and **Fabric** loaders.

Transform your Minecraft experience into an exciting, unpredictable adventure! From randomized crop harvests and custom death drops to structure chest loot, continuous smelting, mob spawns, and crafting recipes — every aspect of the game can be independently toggled and tuned in real time!

---

## ✨ Features at a Glance

- 🌾 **Random Crop Drops**: Harvesting any farm crop (Wheat, Carrots, Potatoes, Beetroots, Nether Wart, Cocoa, Sweet Berry Bushes, Melons, Pumpkins, Sugar Cane, Cactus, Torchflowers, Pitcher Plants) yields randomized loot items.
- 💀 **Random Death Drops**: Items dropped from your inventory upon death transform into random items from the global item pool.
- 📦 **Random Chest Loot**: Dungeon, village, nether fortress, end city, minecart, and structure chests fill with randomized loot across all dimensions.
- ⛏️ **Random Mining Drops**: Breaking blocks in the world drops randomized items.
- ⚔️ **Random Mob Drops**: Slaying mobs drops randomized items from the pool.
- 🔨 **Random Crafting Outputs**: Crafting recipes yield randomized results.
- 🔥 **Random Bulk Smelting**: Furnaces, blast furnaces, and smokers output randomized items during continuous bulk smelting.
- 🎣 **Random Fishing Loot**: Reeling in your fishing line yields unexpected fish, treasure, or junk items.
- 🧱 **Random Block Placement**: Placing a block transforms it into a random block type in the world.
- 🐷 **Random Entity Spawns**: Natural and mob spawner spawns replace intended mobs with random mob types.
- 🧪 **Random Potion Brewing**: Brewing stands produce randomized potion effects and items.

## 🖥️ Modern In-Game Dashboard & Features

Press **`R`** in-game or type `/randomizer` to open the built-in **Universal Randomizer Dashboard**:

### 🔍 JEI / REI Style Item Picker
- **Live Search Bar**: Filter items instantly by ID (e.g. `minecraft:diamond`) or display name.
- **Interactive 3D Grid**: Browse registered items in a 10x6 grid with real-time hover tooltips.
- **Pagination**: Navigate through item pages effortlessly using `< Prev` and `Next >` controls.

### ⚡ One-Click Preset Profiles
Easily select pre-configured playstyles:
- **Chaos Default**: Pure randomized mayhem across all major modes.
- **Classic Randomizer**: Traditional mining drops, mob drops, and chest loot randomization.
- **Peaceful Farmer**: Relaxed gameplay with randomized crop drops, fishing loot, and smelting.
- **World Craze**: Randomized block placement, entity spawns, and death drops.

## ⌨️ Controls & Commands

### Keybind
- **`R`** (Default): Opens the Universal Randomizer Hub Dashboard. (Configurable in `Options -> Controls -> Key Binds -> Universal Randomizer`).

### Commands
- `/randomizer` or `/randomizer gui` — Opens the interactive dashboard.
- `/randomizer toggle <mode>` — Toggles a specific randomizer mode on or off.
- `/randomizer preset <name>` — Loads a preset profile.
- `/randomizer seed set <seed>` — Sets a custom seed for deterministic world randomization.
- `/randomizer reload` — Reloads configuration files.

## ⚙️ Compatibility & Performance

- **Platforms**: Supports **Forge** (47.4.16+) and **Fabric** (0.92.6+).
- **Modpack Friendly**: Fully compatible with popular modpack frameworks, **JEI**, **REI**, **EMI**, **GeckoLib**, **Sodium**, **Embeddium**, and **Sinytra Connector**.
- **Crash Prevention**: Features built-in thread safety (`ThreadLocal` flags) and projectile filters to prevent conflicts with weapon/gun mods (*Just Enough Guns*, *BBS*).

## 📜 Installation

1. Download the version corresponding to your mod loader (**Forge** or **Fabric**).
2. Place the `.jar` file into your `.minecraft/mods` folder.
3. Launch Minecraft, press **`R`**, and start randomizing!
